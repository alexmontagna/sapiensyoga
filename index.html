what's sapiens yoga all about?

Sapiens yoga is an approach built on the classic Indian techniques.

It teaches the tradition while embracing modern human knowledge.

why choose sapiens yoga?

It's a simple, strong and pleasant practice, for adult humans who want to become flexible, with a clear mind and a heart at peace.

what's the method like?

What makes it unique is how a class is built. You perform free body exercises, train breathing techniques and practise mindfulness along the way, with precious moments of meditation. The whole flow brings inner clarity, and a better understanding of your thoughts and emotions.

You develop organic happiness and a sense of connection: you feel good, in the right place and at the right time.

who's behind sapiens yoga?

Alex, Alexandre Montagna, who created the method and teaches it in Milan. That's him in the photo above, sitting cross-legged among the musicians.
